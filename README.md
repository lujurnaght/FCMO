# Fractional CMO

A training ground for becoming a top-1% fractional CMO: study, spar, and do the real work.

- **Learn** — 27 lessons in 9 tracks, with spaced-repetition review.
- **Spar** — AI role-plays (discovery, objections, board, negotiation…) and a daily case, graded by an AI panel that shows you the elite answer. Uses your free Groq key.
- **Field** — log real outreach, posts, calls and proposals; run your pipeline; claim quests with proof.
- **Rank** — XP gets you far, but the upper ranks only unlock with real clients.
